SECTION 03 30 00 - CAST-IN-PLACE CONCRETE
Project: Riverside Medical Pavilion (vConstruct sample project)

PART 1 - GENERAL

1.1 SUMMARY
A. This Section covers footings, foundation walls, slabs-on-grade, elevated slabs, columns and shear walls.

1.2 REFERENCES
A. ACI 301 - Specifications for Concrete Construction.
B. ASTM C39 - Compressive Strength of Cylindrical Concrete Specimens.
C. ASTM C143 - Slump of Hydraulic-Cement Concrete.
D. ASTM C231 - Air Content of Freshly Mixed Concrete by the Pressure Method.

1.3 SUBMITTALS
A. Mix designs: Submit proposed mix designs for each class of concrete at least 14 calendar days before the first placement.
B. Test reports: Submit compressive strength reports to the Architect within 3 working days of each test.
C. Reinforcement shop drawings: Submit bar lists and placing drawings before fabrication.

1.4 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. An independent testing agency shall sample concrete in accordance with ASTM C172.
B. Cast four cylinders for each 50 cubic yards, or fraction thereof, placed in a day. Test one cylinder at 7 days and two at 28 days (ASTM C39); hold one in reserve.

PART 2 - PRODUCTS

2.1 CONCRETE MIXES
A. Footings and foundation walls: 4,000 psi minimum compressive strength at 28 days; maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.50.
B. Slabs-on-grade: 4,000 psi at 28 days; maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.45; slump 4 inches plus or minus 1 inch.
C. Elevated slabs and columns: 5,000 psi minimum compressive strength at 28 days; maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.42.
D. Exterior exposed concrete: air-entrained, 6 percent plus or minus 1.5 percent total air content (ASTM C231).

2.2 REINFORCEMENT
A. Deformed bars: ASTM A615, Grade 60.
B. Welded wire reinforcement: ASTM A1064.

PART 3 - EXECUTION

3.1 PLACEMENT
A. Do not place concrete when the ambient temperature is below 40 degrees F or above 90 degrees F unless a cold-weather or hot-weather plan has been approved by the Architect.
B. Discharge concrete within 90 minutes after batching.

3.2 CURING AND PROTECTION
A. Moist-cure concrete for a minimum of 7 days, or apply a curing compound complying with ASTM C309.
B. In cold weather, maintain concrete temperature at or above 50 degrees F for the first 72 hours after placement.

3.3 TOLERANCES
A. Slab floor flatness and levelness: FF 35 and FL 25 overall, measured within 72 hours of placement.
