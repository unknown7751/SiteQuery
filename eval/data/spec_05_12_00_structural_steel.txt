SECTION 05 12 00 - STRUCTURAL STEEL FRAMING
Project: Riverside Medical Pavilion (SiteQuery sample project)

PART 1 - GENERAL

1.1 SUMMARY
A. This Section covers wide-flange beams and columns, HSS members, base plates, and connections.

1.2 REFERENCES
A. AISC 360 - Specification for Structural Steel Buildings.
B. AWS D1.1 - Structural Welding Code - Steel.
C. ASTM A992 - Structural Steel Shapes.
D. ASTM F3125 - High-Strength Structural Bolts.

1.3 SUBMITTALS
A. Shop drawings: Submit shop drawings and erection drawings sealed by a licensed structural engineer. Allow 10 working days for review of each submittal.
B. Mill certificates: Submit mill test reports for all structural shapes and plates.
C. Welder certifications: Submit AWS certifications for every welder, current within 6 months.
D. Bolt certifications: Submit manufacturer certificates for all high-strength bolts and tension-control assemblies.

PART 2 - PRODUCTS

2.1 MATERIALS
A. Wide-flange shapes: ASTM A992, Grade 50, with a minimum yield strength of 50 ksi.
B. HSS rectangular and square sections: ASTM A500, Grade C.
C. Plates and angles: ASTM A36.
D. High-strength bolts: ASTM F3125 Grade A325, snug-tight unless noted as pretensioned.

PART 3 - EXECUTION

3.1 ERECTION
A. Verify anchor rod locations and elevations before erection. Report discrepancies to the Architect by RFI.
B. Maintain a minimum of 3/4 inch bearing on base plates after grouting.

3.2 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL
A. Inspect 100 percent of complete-joint-penetration welds by ultrasonic testing.
B. Inspect pretensioned bolted joints in accordance with the RCSC Specification.
