## RFI-0112 — Control Joint Spacing, P2 Slab-on-Grade

**Question:** Drawing S-201 shows control joints at 15 ft on center; spec 03 30 00 §3.6 states 12 ft
maximum. Which governs?

**Response (Meridian Engineers, 2025-02-21):** Spec governs. Saw-cut joints at **12 ft maximum** each
way, depth one-quarter of slab thickness, cut within 12 hours of finishing. S-201 to be revised in
Addendum 4.

## RFI-0127 — Rebar Conflict at Transfer Beam, Gridline C/4

**Question:** Top bars of transfer beam TB-3 collide with the column dowels at C/4. Bar spacing
cannot satisfy both details.

**Response (Meridian Engineers, 2025-03-07):** Shift the column dowels 2 in. toward the beam
centerline and use #9 bars at 5 in. spacing in the beam top layer. Cost impact: **none claimed** by
Ridgeline. Schedule impact: 1 day.

## RFI-0134 — Mix Design for 8,000 psi Columns

**Question:** Confirm the approved mix for columns on Levels 1-6, and whether supplementary
cementitious materials are permitted.

**Response (Meridian Engineers, 2025-03-14):** Use mix **C-8000-A**: 8,000 psi at 56 days,
w/cm 0.32 maximum, slump 8 in. ± 1 in. after HRWR. Fly ash up to 25% by mass of cementitious
material and slag up to 40% are permitted. Submit trial batch results before the first placement.

## RFI-0141 — Sleeve Penetrations Through PT Slab, Level 4

**Question:** Mechanical has asked for eleven 8-in. round sleeves and two 14 in. x 10 in. rectangular
openings through the Level 4 post-tensioned slab. Several fall within 24 in. of tendons banded along
Gridline D.

**Status:** **OPEN.** Awaiting EOR response; the response is now 11 days past due. Level 4 slab pour
is scheduled for 2025-04-22, so the sleeves must be located and coordinated by 2025-04-18. GPR
scanning was proposed by the GC as a fallback. Ridgeline has been told not to place sleeves near
the banded tendons until written direction is received.

## RFI-0149 — Cold-Weather Concrete Placement, Level 5 Deck

**Question:** Overnight lows are forecast below 40 °F during the Level 5 pour window. What
protection measures does the EOR require?

**Status:** **OPEN.** Preliminary guidance from Meridian: maintain concrete temperature at or above
**50 °F** during placement and at least **55 °F for the first 48 hours** (not exceeding 70 °F). Insulated
blankets are required; salamander heaters must be vented outdoors. Formal response is pending.

## RFI-0153 — Embed Plate Tolerance at Shear Wall SW-2

**Question:** Survey shows 5 of 12 embed plates at SW-2 are up to 1-1/4 in. out of position
horizontally. Is the specified tolerance of ± 1/2 in. enforceable, or can the steel detailer adjust?

**Response (Meridian Engineers, 2025-04-11):** Plates beyond ± 1/2 in. require the steel fabricator to
submit revised connection details for EOR review. Plates within 1 in. may be accepted with slotted
holes and a 3/8 in. oversized washer plate. Plates beyond 1 in. must be corrected. The two outliers
(E-7, E-9) at 1-1/4 in. need rework. Awaiting GC close-out.
