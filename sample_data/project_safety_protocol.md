# Project Safety Protocol — Harbor Point Mixed-Use Tower

**Document No.:** HSE-001 · **Revision:** C · **Issued:** 2025-02-10
**Prepared by:** J. Alvarez, Site Safety Manager · **Approved by:** D. Okafor, Project Executive

> Sample document for demonstration only. Project, people and values are fictional.

## 1. Scope

This protocol applies to all employees, subcontractors, and visitors on the Harbor Point site
(Levels P2 through Roof). Where this protocol and OSHA 29 CFR 1926 differ, the more stringent
requirement governs.

## 2. Site Access and Orientation

1. Every worker must complete the 2-hour site orientation before receiving a badge.
2. Visitors must sign in at the gate trailer and be escorted at all times.
3. Hard hats, safety glasses (ANSI Z87.1), high-visibility vests (Class 2), and steel-toe boots are
   mandatory beyond the gate. Class 3 vests are required for work within 15 ft of active traffic.

## 3. Fall Protection

- Fall protection is required at **6 feet** or more above a lower level, and at any height over
  rebar, equipment, or open excavations.
- Guardrails are the preferred method. Top rails shall be 42 in. (± 3 in.) above the walking surface;
  mid-rails at 21 in.
- Personal fall arrest systems (PFAS) must be inspected before each use and anchored to a point rated
  for **5,000 lb** per attached worker.
- Floor and roof openings 2 in. or larger must be covered with plywood rated for twice the expected
  load and marked "HOLE — COVER". Covers are secured against displacement.
- Leading-edge work on the post-tensioned decks requires a written Fall Protection Plan approved by
  the Site Safety Manager before work starts.

## 4. Excavation and Shoring

- A competent person inspects excavations daily, after rain, and after any change in conditions.
- Trench protection is required at depths of **5 feet** or greater unless in stable rock.
- Ladders or ramps must be provided within 25 ft of lateral travel for trenches 4 ft or deeper.
- Spoil piles stay at least 2 ft from the edge of the excavation.

## 5. Hot Work

1. A hot work permit is required for welding, cutting, brazing, and grinding outside the designated
   shop area. Permits are valid for one shift only.
2. A fire watch must remain in place during the work and for **30 minutes** afterwards.
3. A 10 lb ABC extinguisher must be within 35 ft of the work.
4. Combustibles are removed or shielded within 35 ft; hot work is prohibited within 50 ft of
   fuel storage.

## 6. Concrete and Formwork

- Reshoring and stripping of formwork may proceed only after the Engineer of Record approves the
  break results (see spec section 03 30 00).
- Workers must not stand under loads being placed by the concrete bucket or pump boom.
- Rebar caps (mushroom caps or wooden troughs) are required on all exposed vertical rebar ends
  that are below 6 ft.
- Silica exposure controls (wet cutting or HEPA vacuum shrouds) are required when cutting or
  grinding cured concrete.

## 7. Cranes and Rigging

- The tower crane operator holds a current NCCCO certification; a copy is kept in the crane cab.
- Critical lifts (over 75% of rated capacity, or personnel lifts) require a lift plan signed by the
  Superintendent and the crane operator.
- Lifting operations stop when sustained winds reach **25 mph** or gusts exceed **35 mph**.
- No loads are carried over occupied buildings or the public right-of-way.

## 8. Incident Reporting

| Event | Report to | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fatality or in-patient hospitalization | Site Safety Manager, then OSHA | Immediately; OSHA within 8 hours |
| Recordable injury | Site Safety Manager | Within 1 hour |
| Near miss | Foreman → Safety Manager | End of shift |
| Property or utility damage | Superintendent | Immediately |

Emergency contacts are posted at the gate trailer and on every hoist landing. The nearest trauma
center is Harborview Medical, 4.2 miles from site.

## 9. Toolbox Talks and Audits

- A 10-minute toolbox talk is held each morning by the foreman of every active trade.
- The Site Safety Manager performs a documented site walk **weekly**; a third-party audit takes
  place **quarterly**.
- Stop-work authority belongs to every person on site, without retaliation.
